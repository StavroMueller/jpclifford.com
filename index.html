02 · atelier actara

the show runs through here.

show management for the modern atelier. a platform for producing fashion shows: casting models, booking photographers and crew, running the schedule, billing, and the chaos of the hour before the doors open. i founded it, i run it, and i wrote most of it.

the platform

  • backend. elixir/phoenix umbrella with absinthe graphql. domain apps for shows, organizations, participants, users, billing, guests, delivery, and imports. oban for background jobs, stripe for payments, postgres underneath.
  • web. react 19, apollo client, mui. multi-tenant, with a standalone superadmin back office deployed on fly.io.
  • ios. native swift app for talent: models, crew, and media. around twenty thousand lines.
  • android. jetpack compose, apollo kotlin, hilt.
  • tooling. an admin cli that speaks to the same graphql api, so business logic lives in exactly one place.
  • ai team. an autonomous council of ceo, pm, cto, dev, qa and coo agents that runs sprint loops against the repo, coordinating through shared files.

03 · at sea

i crew these ships; i do not visit them.

elissa is a three-masted iron barque built in aberdeen in 1877 and sailed today by volunteer crew out of galveston, texas. night watches, make ready, day sails in the gulf. in june 2026 she left the gulf for the atlantic and i went with her.

the 2026 passage, from the log

  1. 20 junrounding cape hatteras. "the graveyard of the atlantic." the most intense sailing of the trip.
  2. 23 junleaving cambridge, maryland. motored off the dock, up chesapeake bay in foul-weather gear, and dropped anchor off annapolis as the town swarmed out in small boats.
  3. 25 juna day of tacking. sailed on and off the anchor, three and a half tacks on a 150-year-old barque, each one five minutes of orchestral precision. then dishes for an hour.
  4. 25 junswim call. furled the fore squares save the royal, jacob's ladder over the side, and jumped off the head deck into the bay. the goonies projected on the mizzen stays'l.
  5. 27 junchesapeake city, under the bridge, in light rain.
  6. 29 junwaited out the current in the c&d canal, fired a cannon crossing into delaware, out through delaware bay and back into the atlantic. then up the rig at sunrise to unfurl, and one knot at a time up the new jersey coast. next stop, new york.

onlytallships.com

the ship's log, written underway and published as an age-of-sail periodical. built with astro, with a leaflet chart of every geotagged dispatch, rss, a guestbook, and a little browser-based editor for writing entries from a bunk with bad signal.

read the log

what it taught me

a tack takes forty people and five minutes. nobody is the hero. the work is mostly done by hands, and most of it happens before anyone is watching.

04 · photography

fashion shows from backstage, and life aboard tall ships. the hour before the doors open: fittings, makeup, last alterations, models waiting in clothes they cannot sit down in. shot at new york fashion week and in houston, since 2018. more than ninety runway shows.
