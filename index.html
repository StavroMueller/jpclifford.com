03 · Atelier Actara

The show runs through here.

Show management for fashion: casting models, booking photographers and crew, running the lineup and the venue, guest lists, and check-in. Agencies, brands, and venues work the same show without seeing each other's data. The first commit was in December 2022 and it became its own company in June 2026.

What it is made of

  • Backend. An Elixir and Phoenix umbrella with Absinthe GraphQL. Separate apps for shows, organizations, participants, users, billing, guests, delivery, and imports. Oban runs background jobs, Stripe handles payments, Postgres holds the data.
  • Web. React 19, Apollo Client, and MUI. A standalone superadmin back office runs on Fly.io.
  • iOS. A native Swift app for talent: models, crew, and media. About twenty thousand lines.
  • Android. Jetpack Compose, Apollo Kotlin, and Hilt, against the same GraphQL schema.
  • CLI. An admin tool that talks to the same API, so validation and business logic live in one place.
  • Agents. A set of AI agents (CEO, PM, CTO, dev, QA, COO) that run sprint loops against the repository and coordinate through shared files.

04 · At sea

Crew aboard Elissa

Elissa is a three-masted iron barque built in Aberdeen in 1877 and sailed today by volunteer crew out of Galveston. I stand watches, work aloft, and handle sail on day sails in the Gulf. In June 2026 she sailed from the Gulf up the Atlantic coast to New York and I was aboard.

The 2026 passage, from the log

  1. 20 JunRounded Cape Hatteras. The heaviest sailing of the trip.
  2. 23 JunLeft Cambridge, Maryland under motor, went up Chesapeake Bay in rain, and anchored off Annapolis. The town came out in small boats to look at us.
  3. 25 JunSailed off the anchor, then spent the day practicing: three and a half tacks, each one about five minutes of coordinated work on a 150-year-old barque. We anchored again that evening and did dishes for an hour.
  4. 25 JunSwim call. Furled the fore squares except the royal, put the Jacob's ladder over the side, and jumped off the head deck into the bay. The Goonies was projected on the mizzen staysail that night.
  5. 27 JunChesapeake City, under the bridge, in light rain.
  6. 29 JunWaited out the current in the C&D Canal, fired a cannon crossing into Delaware, and went out through Delaware Bay into the Atlantic. The next morning I went aloft at sunrise to unfurl, and we made one knot up the New Jersey coast toward New York.

onlytallships.com

The ship's log from that passage, written underway and laid out like a nineteenth-century periodical. It is built with Astro and has a Leaflet chart of every geotagged entry, an RSS feed, a guestbook, and a small browser-based editor for writing entries from a bunk with poor signal.

Rod Tips Only

Five fishing rods on a dock in Louisiana, rendered in three.js, where sometimes a fish bites. Each rod is a tapered cantilever beam simulated as a chain of rigid links with torsion springs and solved with XPBD, so a redfish bends it the way a redfish would. Ships as a PWA with a replay system, catch cards, and a depression meter.
