03 · Atelier Actara

The show runs through here.

Atelier Actara manages fashion shows: it casts models, books photographers and crew, runs the lineup and the venue, and handles guest lists and check-in. Agencies, brands, and venues work the same show without seeing each other's data. I made the first commit in December 2022 and it became its own company in June 2026.

What it is made of

  • Backend. The back end is an Elixir and Phoenix umbrella with Absinthe GraphQL, split into apps for shows, organizations, participants, users, billing, guests, delivery, and imports. Oban runs background jobs, Stripe handles payments, and Postgres holds the data.
  • Web. The web app uses React 19, Apollo Client, and MUI, and a standalone superadmin back office runs on Fly.io.
  • iOS. The iOS app is native Swift, about twenty thousand lines, built for models, crew, and media.
  • Android. The Android app uses Jetpack Compose, Apollo Kotlin, and Hilt against the same GraphQL schema.
  • CLI. The admin CLI talks to the same API, so validation and business logic live in one place.
  • Agents. A set of AI agents playing CEO, PM, CTO, dev, QA, and COO run sprint loops against the repository and coordinate through shared files.

04 · At sea

Crew aboard Elissa

Elissa is a three-masted iron barque built in Aberdeen in 1877 and sailed today by volunteer crew out of Galveston. I stand watches, work aloft, and handle sail on day sails in the Gulf. In June 2026 she sailed from the Gulf up the Atlantic coast to New York and I was aboard.

The 2026 passage, from the log

  1. 20 JunWe rounded Cape Hatteras, which was the heaviest sailing of the trip.
  2. 23 JunWe left Cambridge, Maryland under motor, went up Chesapeake Bay in rain, and anchored off Annapolis, where the town came out in small boats to look at us.
  3. 25 JunWe sailed off the anchor and spent the day practicing: three and a half tacks, each one about five minutes of coordinated work on a 150-year-old barque. We anchored again that evening and did dishes for an hour.
  4. 25 JunWe furled the fore squares except the royal, put the Jacob's ladder over the side, and jumped off the head deck into the bay for a swim call. That night The Goonies was projected on the mizzen staysail.
  5. 27 JunWe lay at Chesapeake City, under the bridge, in light rain.
  6. 29 JunWe waited out the current in the C&D Canal, fired a cannon crossing into Delaware, and went out through Delaware Bay into the Atlantic. The next morning I went aloft at sunrise to unfurl, and we made one knot up the New Jersey coast toward New York.

onlytallships.com

I wrote the ship's log for that passage underway and published it at onlytallships.com, laid out like a nineteenth-century periodical. It is built with Astro and has a Leaflet chart of every geotagged entry, an RSS feed, a guestbook, and a small browser-based editor for writing entries from a bunk with poor signal.

2023 to 2026 · JavaScript · three.js

Rod Tips Only

Rod Tips Only puts five fishing rods on a dock in Louisiana and waits for a bite. I model each rod as a tapered cantilever beam, a chain of rigid links joined by torsion springs and solved with XPBD, so a redfish bends it the way a redfish would. It runs as a PWA and has a replay system, catch cards, and a depression meter.
